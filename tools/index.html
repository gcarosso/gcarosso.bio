SAROS
SAROS compares programs, not molecules. A public ledger of clinical readouts—dates, sponsors, signals. Filterable landscape for FY26-28 (area × phase × modality, target, geography, sponsor domicile) with transparent heuristics and contextual financing signals, plus distinct longevity and private-sponsor slices. Strictly public-data and non-advisory, updates daily.
Built by Giovanni Carosso

deep-ancestry
Ancestral narrative over static compositions. A deeper dive into ancestral shape questions like who arrived when, from where, in what order; from consumer genotype raw data files. Browser-based, no server, no build, no dependencies.
Built by Giovanni Carosso
Open ↗·Source·License: MIT
Pileup
Visualizer for agent calls. Pileup visually lays out the tool calls, retries, subagents, and context changes so you can inspect how the work happened. Not quite mechanistic interpretability, but it's something.
