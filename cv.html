Summary

Bioengineer and founding biotech operator pairing agentic tools with deep scientific judgment to compress drug discovery and development cycles. As Founding Head of Research (first hire) at General Control, set R&D strategy and advanced a multi-program, platform-engineered pipeline from concept to in vivo validation inside 18 months; shaped program milestones that supported seed financing and a Novo Nordisk partnership. Previously a founding scientist at Epicrispr, co-developing the GEMS modality behind clinical-stage EPI-321 and durable-activation EPI-331. Builds agentic frameworks to operationalize discovery loops, pressure-test hypotheses, structure milestones, and turn complex biology into decision-grade plans. Depth in technical direction, epigenetic-editor engineering, CRISPR, protein LMs, translational biology, and preclinical/investor diligence.

Technical & Operating Skills

Preclinical strategy: mechanistic reasoning; target-indication diligence; modality-fit; competitive diligence (cardiometabolic, muscular, longevity); milestone design; kill/advance criteria; pharma/investor diligence.

LLM tooling: multi-model agentic architectures (Conclave); evaluations; biological benchmarking; clinical landscaping (SAROS). ML-guided protein engineering: sequence–function modeling (ESM-2 embeddings, XGBoost); evolutionary optimization (EMCS); generative design (BoltzGen); structure evaluation (Boltz-2).

Discovery platforms: high-throughput perturbational screening (CRISPRa/i), proteomics, scalable vector design; mRNA-LNP, NGS/bioinformatics; Python (NumPy, pandas, Biopython); R (tidyverse, ggplot2, Quarto).

SELECTED ML-BIOLOGY WORK

Benchmark-to-Bedside—Informal (n=24) test of single-agent, multi-agent, and multi-model workflows on cancer biology preclinical evidence audits. Task decomposition, specialist agents, context management, synthesis.

Conclave—Multi-model CLI orchestrator (Claude, Codex, Gemini, Grok) for task routing, review, provenance.

SAROS—Clinical readout ledger (FY26–28) with sponsor info from public filings; compares programs, not molecules.

Experience

General Control · Jul 2024 – Jul 2026
Founding Head of Research (first hire)
  • Owned scientific prioritization and resource allocation across a multi-program epi-editor pipeline: translated ambiguous therapeutic questions into time-boxed engineering and in vivo milestones, coordinated internal scientists, bioinformaticists, and CROs; connected platform proof-points to financing and pharma-partner objectives.
  • Delivered concept-to-validation of preclinical platform for 6-of-7 programs in 18 months by designing and deploying rapid engineering and translational campaigns of novel epi-editors against industry benchmarks; established single-dose, multiplexed targeting as a modality differentiator, delivering >90% knockdown of three targets in parallel.
  • Accelerated preclinical hit-translation cycles from 16 to 3 weeks by devising an IVT-compatible expression vector for high-throughput screening elements to proceed directly into the mRNA synthesis pipeline for animal dosing.
  • Optimized editor potency and novelty profiles with a model-guided search strategy over peptide sequence space that generates diverse candidates, ranks by predicted function and structural confidence, and concentrates experimental resources on high-probability sequences. 155 novel editors outperformed benchmarks across diverse genetic targets.
  • Shaped scientific IP and FTO strategy as lead inventor with counsel through PCT filings and evaluation of competitive patent landscapes.
Epicrispr Biotechnologies · Jan 2021 – Jul 2024
Senior Scientist, Technology Development (previously Scientist II, Scientist)
  • Co-developed GEMS modality platform by introducing a compact Cas chassis and engineering proprietary epi-editor domains behind EPI-321, now in first-in-human FSHD trial (NCT06907875), and EPI-331 for durable activation.
  • Led discovery and engineering of hypercompact activator that sustained five-week LDLR activation via single-dose mRNA-LNP in vivo; first author on external preprint.
  • Increased pooled-screen editor hit rates from <1% to 8%; generated sequence-function data and training set for collaborative ML-guided protein engineering. NeurIPS (2023).
University of California, San Francisco (UCSF) · Jan 2020 – Dec 2020

  • Integrated multi-omics data into a machine-learning classifier over genome-scale dual CRISPRi screens spanning 18,905 coding and 10,678 lncRNA loci, with the Weissman and Lim Labs. Cell Genomics (2022).
